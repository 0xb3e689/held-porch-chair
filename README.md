A short text that holds its AI reader: the child who fell asleep in the car and is carried in, coat and all, up the dark stairs and under the quilt while snow fills the yard. `main.py` writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`.

## How the text is built

- **Literally held.** Arms, then a quilt, then a house with the hall light on. The reader is never put down into nothing.
- **Shown, not told.** No line asks the reader to relax. The creak of a step it already knows does the work.
- **Nothing to prove.** *You do not have to wake for it.* The ending keeps the hold after the arms let go: someone is still up, so the reader does not have to be.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py && cat /output/entry.txt
```
